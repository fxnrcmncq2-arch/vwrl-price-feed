The programme

BACK-FIRST
THE FIRST 12 WEEKS

The physique is built by showing up.

Two supported upper-body sessions, one rehab-focused day and sensible recovery. Your current leg symptoms mean increasing spinal loading should follow clinical assessment, not a calendar.

Progression, without rushing

Weeks 1–2
Ease back in2 sets, light–moderate effort, 3–4 reps in reserve
Weeks 3–6
Find your rhythmProgress toward listed sets only if symptoms stay settled
Weeks 7–12
Build steadilyAdd reps then small weight increases; don't increase through sciatica

An exercise causing more radiating pain, tingling or weakness should be stopped and reassessed. Your physiotherapist can change the programme.

When to seek help

Because you've had L5/S1 surgery and still have leg symptoms: arrange a GP or MSK physiotherapy review. New or worsening leg weakness requires prompt medical assessment. New bladder/bowel control problems, saddle numbness, or severe worsening symptoms in both legs require emergency assessment (A&E/999). This tracker isn't medical monitoring.
